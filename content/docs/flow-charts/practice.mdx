## Steps

1. **Client.** Agrees to the recording of this session, with purpose and duration.
2. **Practice system.** Creates the session, hands over the recording, fetches report and history.
3. **Professional.** Reads the report next to their own notes. Confirms, corrects, discards.
4. **Consent.** Per session.
5. **Perception.** Report on the session.
6. **Memory.** History across sessions, including confirmations and corrections.
7. **Export, deletion.** Right of the client, with receipt to the practice.

<Callout>
  The report is material, not a verdict. Corrections by the professional improve the history and stay visible as corrections.
</Callout>
