## Steps

1. **Family.** Each person is a subject with their own consent. Children: consent of the guardians, narrow purpose.
2. **Robot.** Camera, microphone, speaker. It decides when a moment is captured. No continuous recording.
3. **Voice layer.** The partner's own technology. ONIOKO gives it context as data, it does not change it.
4. **Consent.** Checked before every hand-over. Without it, nothing leaves the robot.
5. **Perception.** Signals per moment. On-device capability is open (fact 14).
6. **Memory.** Across days and weeks, with source. On-device capability is open (fact 14).
7. **Export, deletion.** At the family's request, with receipt.

<Callout>
  Two variants: in the cloud variant chosen moments are handed over with consent. In the on-device variant parts of Perception run on the robot. Which parts can do that today is answered from the code, not on this page.
</Callout>
