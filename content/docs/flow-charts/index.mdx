Every integration partner asks the same first question: where in my system does your layer sit, what goes in, what comes back, who may do what? The answer is a flow chart, not an endpoint.

Click a box to see what happens there, its inputs and its outputs. Arrows carry data. Dashed amber lines mark every place where consent allows or stops an action. The partner's own layers (voice, app, practice system, platform) stay untouched in every scenario.

<FlowChart />

## Reading the charts

- **White boxes** are the partner's systems. ONIOKO never replaces them.
- **Coral boxes** are people. Each is a subject with its own consent.
- **Amber dashed boxes** are consent and trust: allow or block per call, export, deletion with receipt.
- **Mint** is Perception, **violet** is Memory.
- A **red dashed outline** marks parts whose on-device capability is still open (fact 14 on the status page).

Each scenario has its own page with the chart and a step-by-step walkthrough.
