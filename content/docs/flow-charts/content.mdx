## Steps

1. **Creator.** Uploads own takes or releases an original. Decides what to do with the comparison.
2. **Content platform.** Holds videos and scripts, hands over videos bound to a session, brings descriptions back into its own interface.
3. **Original video.** A public post. Description only, no judgement of the person.
4. **Consent.** Creator per take.
5. **Perception.** Delivery per segment, in words.
6. **Memory.** Creator over weeks, only for them.
7. **Side by side.** Take next to original, segment by segment. No score.

<Callout>
  ONIOKO delivers data, no UI. The platform decides how to show it.
</Callout>
