ONIOKO builds **Extended Human Cognition**: technology that perceives observable signals from face, voice and body over time, and keeps what it perceived as memory with its source intact. Products are built on top of it. Partners integrate it.

Every content or conversation tool today reads two things: the text and the numbers. ONIOKO adds a third reading, the person in the video or in the room, and a memory that holds these impressions together over weeks. The human always keeps the interpretation.

## The four layers

| Layer | What it does | For partners |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Perception** | Reads observable signals from face, voice and body, without labels. | Submit or stream consented inputs, receive measured signals or an unavailable state. |
| **Memory** | Connects the current context with long-term memory, source and provenance intact. | Write and retrieve person-bound records. |
| **Cognition** | Places memory next to current questions, sources stay visible. | Used inside ONIOKO products today. |
| **Presence** | Extended Avatars with face, voice and continuity across conversations. | Used inside ONIOKO products today. |

## What ONIOKO does not do

- No emotion labels. Signals are described, never named as feelings.
- No scoring of people, no ranking, no population scoring.
- No medical or psychological diagnosis.
- No continuous recording. Only chosen moments, with consent that can be revoked and leads to deletion.

These are not a legal footnote. They are constraints built into every call: without valid consent for a subject, Perception answers "unavailable" and Memory writes nothing.
